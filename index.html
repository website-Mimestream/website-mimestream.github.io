Mimestream for Mac

A native macOS email client for Gmail. Combines your favorite Gmail features with the power of a native app, so you move through email effortlessly. Made for Mac, optimized for Gmail.

Lightning-Fast Performance

Built in Swift, Mimestream leverages macOS for a familiar, instant experience. Uses the Gmail API—not IMAP—for enhanced speed and features. Lightweight, with minimal disk usage for recent messages.

Categorized Inbox & Organization

Rise above the noise. Focus on what matters with Gmail categories. Manage multiple accounts and labels effortlessly. Swipe gestures and keyboard shortcuts make triage intuitive.

Privacy First

Your data stays on your device. Direct connections to Gmail only—no intermediaries. Blocks tracking pixels from 50+ services. Read privately, without compromise.

Seamless Gmail Integration

Aliases and signatures sync automatically. Robust search works offline. System notifications keep you informed. Fully functional offline or on slow connections.
